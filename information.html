About NexCart

NexCart is a local quick-commerce marketplace that helps customers discover nearby shops and order everyday products. Shops manage their own catalogues, availability and fulfilment, while NexCart provides discovery, ordering and delivery coordination.

Local first

We connect customers with participating shops in their service area.

Fast and transparent

Availability, delivery distance and order progress are shown before completion.

Built for trust

Successful orders and rewards are verified against recorded order status.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 15 July 2026

This policy explains how NexCart handles information when you use its customer, shop or delivery services.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email address and mobile number.
  • Delivery address, approximate or precise location when you grant permission, and order instructions.
  • Cart, order, payment-method selection, rewards and support history.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, timestamps and security logs.

How we use information

  • To authenticate users, show nearby shops, process orders and coordinate delivery.
  • To prevent fraud, protect accounts, troubleshoot problems and comply with law.
  • To calculate genuine rewards from successfully completed orders.
  • To improve service performance and communicate essential order updates.

Sharing

We share only the information needed with the selected shop, delivery partner, infrastructure providers and authorities where legally required. NexCart does not sell personal information.

Storage and security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used. Access should be controlled through authenticated roles and database security rules. No internet system is completely risk-free.

Your choices

You may deny location access and enter an address manually. You may request access, correction or deletion of eligible information by contacting support. Certain transaction records may be retained for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting requirements.

Do not place secret keys, passwords or payment credentials in public profile fields or support messages.

Orders and payment

An order request is not final until accepted and recorded. You must pay the displayed total using an available method. Delivery may be refused when the destination is outside the supported radius, information is incomplete, stock is unavailable or safety cannot be assured.
