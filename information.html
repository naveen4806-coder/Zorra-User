Help & Support

For assistance with an order, open My Orders on the Home page and review its latest status. For account, vendor or delivery support, contact the relevant Zorra administrator through your registered portal.

Privacy

Zorra uses account, address, order and location information only to provide ordering, delivery, support and platform-security services. Access to operational data is restricted through authenticated accounts and deployed Firebase rules.

  • Keep your password private.
  • Review delivery addresses before checkout.
  • Do not share verification codes with anyone.

Terms

Availability, pricing, delivery time and product information are provided by participating local shops. Orders may be cancelled or refunded when products are unavailable, addresses are outside the supported area, or platform safety checks cannot be completed.
